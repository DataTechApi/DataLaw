# Team Membership Rules — DataLaw

## Expectations for Every Member

To remain part of the team, every member is expected to:

- **Be proactive** — identify problems, raise blockers early, and take initiative without waiting to be told what to do.
- **Be independent** — solve problems and move tasks forward with minimal supervision.
- **Have a sense of urgency** — treat deadlines and commitments seriously, and act quickly when something is at risk.
- **Deliver what was committed** — follow through on tasks and stories the member has personally taken on.

## Grounds for Removal from the Team

A member may be removed from the team if they:

- Show **indifference toward the project** (no engagement, no interest in outcomes).
- **Fail to collaborate**, technically or personally, with the rest of the team.
- **Do not attend alignments** (stand-ups, plannings, reviews) on a regular basis.
- **Do not follow the team's methodology** (Scrum ceremonies, DoR/DoD, branching and commit standards, etc.).
